# AI assistance disclosure

This repository's initial implementation was produced with substantial AI assistance under the direction and review of Karen Cohen. AI assistance contributed to application and CLI source code, holiday calculations, tests, project documentation, AppStream and Snap metadata, desktop integration, icon artwork, CI configuration, and release-readiness analysis. The owner must review and take responsibility for each artifact before treating it as an upstream release.

The upstream calendrical calculations are consumed from the separately published `jwcalendar-calendrical` package; that dependency is not represented as newly authored here. This disclosure describes provenance; it does not assert Flathub acceptance or satisfy any later submission-specific disclosure process by itself.

No AI-generated Flatpak manifest or Flathub submission material is included in this repository.
